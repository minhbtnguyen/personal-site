An end-to-end agentic pipeline on Microsoft's GeoLife GPS trajectories. A
LightGBM model infers how people travel from GPS alone, with no extra sensors.
Claude writes each user a carbon report, fact-checked by a Judge–Critic agent
loop and validated against human labels with Cohen's κ.

Fifteen LLM-driven commuters on a social graph then live through a simulated
week under escalating Apple ESG nudges. Car use and CO₂ fell at every level.
