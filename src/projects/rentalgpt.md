Built on Two Sigma's RentHop listing data. Features fuse tabular attributes,
BERT sentiment from listing text, and YOLOv5 detections from listing photos to
predict rent and renter interest.

A Dash app brings listings, analysis, and predictions together with an
open-source LLM chat assistant, deployed with Docker and Kubernetes on GCP.
